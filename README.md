# Abhishek Ganeshappa Attikatti — Digital Marketing Portfolio

A modern, high-converting, single-page portfolio website showcasing the skills, campaign case studies, verified tool stack, and career background of **Abhishek Ganeshappa Attikatti**, a certified Digital Marketing Specialist & Organic Growth Strategist based in Bengaluru.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Features
- **Modern Dark SaaS Design System**: Built with Tailwind CSS, custom glassmorphism effects, and Plus Jakarta Sans typography.
- **Floating Frameless Hero**: Cutout portrait artwork with ambient neon glow.
- **17-Tool Certified Ecosystem**: Verified real-brand SVG icons including Google Search Console, Ahrefs, PageSpeed Insights, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Maps, Canva, Figma, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Screaming Frog.
- **In-Depth Case Studies**: Real campaigns with challenges, strategies, step-by-step execution, and verified ROI metrics.
- **Experience & Education Timeline**: Detailed record of agency internships, Digital Market Academy training, and academic background.
- **Mobile Responsive**: Fully responsive layout optimized across mobile, tablet, and widescreen desktop displays.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5 & Vanilla JavaScript**
- **Tailwind CSS** (CDN)
- **FontAwesome 6** (Icons)
- **Google Fonts** (Inter & Plus Jakarta Sans)
